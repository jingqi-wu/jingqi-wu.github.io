I am a Project Engineer at VisionNav Robotics, where I work on the deployment, integration, and optimization of autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for industrial applications. My role involves project implementation, system integration, technical troubleshooting, and cross-functional collaboration to deliver reliable robotic solutions for customers.

I am a [Computer Science](https://sds.cuhk.edu.cn/en/page/363) graduate from the [Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://sds.cuhk.edu.cn/en), with a strong passion for artificial intelligence, machine learning, and software engineering. I am pursuing a Master's degree in [Information Systems](https://www.bu.edu/met/degrees-certificates/ms-computer-information-systems/) at [Boston University](https://www.bu.edu/), where I focus on software development, database design, and IT strategy.

Previously, I worked as a Software Engineer in [Xiaomi](https://www.mi.com/us/)’s Display Technology Group, where I researched Android display architecture and optimized rendering pipelines to improve system performance. I also served as a Research Assistant on a [reinforcement learning-based video editing](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3581783.3611878) project published at ACM Multimedia (ACMMM) 2023, focusing on integrating vision-language models with automated video editing techniques.

I have experience developing software across a variety of domains, including networking, databases, computer vision, and intelligent systems. My technical skill set includes Python, C/C++, Java, SQL, PyTorch, OpenCV, MATLAB, and MySQL, with a strong interest in software architecture, system optimization, robotics, and artificial intelligence.

I enjoy solving complex engineering challenges and continuously learning new technologies. I am always interested in connecting with professionals in software engineering, robotics, AI, and automation to exchange ideas and explore new opportunities.
